Privacy Policy

Game: Hearth & Haven • Effective Date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hearth & Haven ("we", "our", or "the app") handles data. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping data collection minimal and transparent.

1. Information We Collect

Hearth & Haven does not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number).

However, the game integrates third-party services that automatically collect technical data to serve advertisements, optimize performance, and process analytics. These may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • IP address (used to infer broad geographic location)
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and in-game performance metrics

2. Third-Party Services

The app relies on trusted third-party SDKs to operate and monetize. Each service governs its data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services: Core game distribution, integrity checks, and account linking. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob & Privacy Details

3. Advertising & User Choices

Third-party advertising partners may use device identifiers and cookies to serve ads based on your general interests. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Hearth & Haven is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe personal information from a minor has been collected in error, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date.
